# closet design philosophy

closet should feel like opening your wardrobe. Your clothes get the attention, and choosing an outfit takes as little effort as possible.

## colors

Warm white in light mode and deep charcoal in dark mode keep the background quiet. Black, white, and soft gray give the controls a clear hierarchy. The clothes bring most of the color. Blue points to actions, and red makes the laundry count easy to spot.

## simplicity

Show the garment clearly, leave room around it, and keep each action obvious. Use spacing to group things, with few borders or boxes. Short labels and familiar icons keep the screen easy to scan. Keep text readable and buttons easy to tap.

## personality

Lowercase names give closet a casual voice. Wooden hangers add warmth, and a little sway makes browsing feel natural. Motion should settle quickly and respect Reduce Motion.

## the rule

Every addition should help you see your clothes, choose an outfit, or remember what you've worn. Keep that useful part simple.
